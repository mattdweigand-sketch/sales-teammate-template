# Prospect Messaging

Product facts live in `policy.template.product_name`, `policy.template.product_value`, `policy.template.product_evidence` and `policy.template.product_review_date`.
This file owns messaging guidance. Unconfigured and demo values carry no verified commercial claim.

## Core messaging

Use the configured value and approved evidence from policy. Evaluate it on the customer's actual work and success measure.

## Three reasons to care

Identify one supported capability, its relevance to the recipient's work and a measurable evaluation. Never invent savings, integrations or outcomes.

## Match the angle to the signal and person

Connect one dated observation to the verified owner's responsibility. An incumbent rollout does not establish dissatisfaction. A public commitment does not establish budget.

## Build the outreach

Use one verified observation, one relevant product connection and one easy question. Keep it to a few plain sentences. Do not paste demo wording unchanged.
Example for a fictional source is an operational initiative at Demo Account. Ask how its owner measures the workflow's outcome.

## Explain the search infrastructure when asked

This legacy heading is retained as a routed compatibility section. Explain only the configured product's verified architecture and capabilities when asked.
Do not inherit another seller's architecture, performance numbers or commercial promises.

## Supporting evidence for follow-up

Use the approved evidence supplied during onboarding, with a source, review date and limitations. No source-company benchmarks are distributed.

## Claim boundaries

No unsupported performance, cost, security, integration or availability claims. Describe potential value until the customer's evidence establishes an outcome.
State a privacy-checked adoption fact in one sentence that keeps the meaning of its `policy.prospecting.user_scan.statements` entry.
No identities, counts, activity dates or spend. Eligibility, suppression, exact approval and readback remain stage controls.
