# Prospect ICP

Read the configured product profile and customer fit below. These are fictional demo settings until onboarding.
Salesforce ownership defines scope. A public signal never grants account ownership.

## Company parameters

Read `policy.template.icp` for the configured territory, industries, personas and exclusions.
Replace this through onboarding with the approved territory, size, industries and exclusions. Employee counts do not establish potential users.

## Target verticals and industries

Demo example is business services. Configure priorities from your actual territory and product evidence. Rankings do not establish conversion performance.

## Target personas

Find the functional owner, technical evaluator, economic buyer and procurement or security owner where applicable.

### Initiative owners

Verify who owns the named initiative and delivery. A title or public post alone does not establish authority.

### Functional sponsors

Find the leader of the affected team. Confirm the workflow, current process, success measure and constraints during discovery.

## Pursuit boundaries

Keep missing budget, authority, timing and required source access explicit. Do not pursue outside configured territory or bypass unmet mandatory requirements.
