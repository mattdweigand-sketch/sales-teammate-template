# Coaching improvement

## Signals

Keep lessons in the Coach thread with dated evidence links. Compare deal outcomes with earlier scores and warnings, including missed risks and warnings that did not hold up.
Record Operator's reactions to coaching, new risk moments, and the Friday system review. Separate a useful coaching lesson from an isolated or unsupported impression.
Use linked deal-thread evidence to verify the sequence. A later outcome alone does not prove an earlier warning was justified or a recommendation caused the result.

## Monthly refresh

At the slot in `policy.coach.schedule.criteria_refresh`, review signals since the previous refresh and propose numbered edits to this workflow's references.
Also prepare a Patterns section for the previous calendar month, separate from the since-last-refresh window for criteria edits. State the month's dates in the refresh schedule's timezone.
Read owned Opportunity call Tasks and Next_Steps__c, Momentum transcripts, and linked deal evidence for that month per `rules#momentum`, `policy.momentum`, and `policy.tooling`.
Respect supported retrieval windows and limits. Name unread or out-of-lookback evidence as not checked, never as an absence of patterns.
Cover objections, blockers, and product gaps recurring on two or more distinct deals, not merely repeated calls on one deal. Give at most 10 patterns.
For each pattern, give the accounts, one dated piece of evidence per deal, and a suggested owner from product, security, deal desk, or enablement.
Post Patterns on the scheduled refresh even when no criteria edit is supported. Post nothing only when there are neither patterns nor supported edits.
Patterns report evidence and suggested owners, not criteria changes or approval to execute work. Account names and buyer quotes stay in the Coach thread, never the repo.
Read only the targeted sections routed in the contract's Inputs, then name the file and heading, exact wording change, evidence links, expected improvement, and counterexamples or uncertainty.
Propose no change without evidence. Generalize lessons into patterns and checks before proposing repo text. Keep customer, company, and person names and buyer quotes out of the repo.
Changes to shared policy values or rules are separate requests for Operator's approval, not reference edits disguised as criteria updates.

## Approval and delivery

Operator approves specific proposal numbers in the Coach thread. Approval covers only the displayed wording. A revised proposal needs a new approval.
Send the approved diff and Coach-thread approval link to Systems repo-maintenance for a repo PR with the required tests and review.
The receiving contract is `workspaces/systems/workflows/repo-maintenance/CONTEXT.md`. Route the handoff without loading or executing that contract during coaching.
The coach never edits or merges repository files. The approved diff ships through that PR under the repository's approval process.
Once merged, the updated references are the canonical criteria for later coaching runs. Reports for Operator remain in the Coach thread and do not require this PR handoff.
Keep the proposal, approval, evidence, and PR link in the Coach thread. Never create a lessons file, run log, or other state file in the repo.
