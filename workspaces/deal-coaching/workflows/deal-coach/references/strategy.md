# Deal strategy

## Strategy questions

Answer each question in one or two sentences with evidence. Mark unsupported answers unknown.

1. Why change, why now, and why this product. Which case is weakest?
2. Power map. Who can say yes, who can say no, and who is missing? Count distinct contacts with Salesforce Contact Roles. One contact means the deal depends on one relationship.
3. Champion risk. What happens if the champion leaves or goes quiet? Identify a verified backup relationship.
4. Wedge. Which workflow wins the first yes? Test whether it is a completed research task with citations rather than a chat replacement.
5. Competitive frame. Can the product complement the incumbent? Establish whether the buyer compares drafting with completed work across sources that includes citations.
6. Hard boundaries. Check residency, certification level, egress allowlisting, buyer-managed keys, connector permissions, and regulated quality systems. Has the rep disclosed limits early?
7. Commercial path. Is there a dated pilot-to-paid conversion step and a seat and credit sizing basis? Check price discipline before discounting unproven value.
8. Adoption truth. Is pilot usage real or only seed provisioning? An unused pilot does not establish a qualified deal.
9. Mutual plan. Is there a buyer-agreed sequence of dated steps to signature? What next step creates one if it is missing?
10. Next best move. What single action this week has the most deal impact, and who should be involved?
